💡 Key Framework

The HEART Framework

Click each letter to explore what it means in practice.

H — Hear

Listen without interrupting. Let the customer finish fully before responding.

E — Empathize

Acknowledge their feelings genuinely. "I completely understand why that's frustrating."

A — Apologize

Offer a sincere apology even if it wasn't your fault. Own the experience.

R — Resolve

Take decisive action. Tell them exactly what you'll do and by when.

T — Thank
Always thank the customer for bringing the issue to your attention. It reframes the complaint as a gift.

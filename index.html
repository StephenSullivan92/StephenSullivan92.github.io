About Me

Teaching shaped how I think about learning design

Five years in the high school ELA classroom taught me that the best instruction doesn't just deliver content — it removes barriers, builds confidence, and makes the "why" impossible to miss. I carry that philosophy into every learning experience I design.

I specialize in applying the ADDIE model, Bloom's Taxonomy, and adult learning theory to design training programs that are measurable, scalable, and genuinely engaging — whether that's corporate onboarding, e-learning modules, or professional development for fellow educators.

Design Philosophy

🎯

Objectives first, always

Every design decision is anchored to a measurable learning objective. If it doesn't serve the outcome, it doesn't make the cut.

🧠

Learner-centered empathy

I conduct needs assessments and analyze learner data before touching a slide. Real design starts with real understanding.

📊

Measure and iterate

Training that can't be evaluated can't be improved. I build assessment and feedback loops into every deliverable.

⚡

Engagement as a strategy

Passive learners don't retain. I design for active participation — scenarios, reflection prompts, and purposeful interaction.

New Employee Onboarding Program
Fictional client: Meridian Financial Group
AudienceNew hires (all departments)
Duration3-day blended program
DeliveryILT + e-learning + job aids

The Challenge

New hires at Meridian were reaching 90-day reviews without understanding core compliance requirements or company culture — leading to costly performance gaps and early turnover. The existing onboarding was a 4-hour slide deck read aloud by HR.

My Solution

Redesigned onboarding as a 3-day blended experience combining a self-paced e-learning pre-work module, two facilitated workshops, and a digital job aid library accessible post-training.

  • Reduced time-to-productivity by an estimated 25% through structured pre-work
  • Created 12 role-specific job aids covering top compliance scenarios
  • Designed a 10-question Level 1 & 2 evaluation aligned to Kirkpatrick
  • Facilitated 2 pilot cohorts and iterated based on learner feedback data
💻 E-Learning
Analyze Design Develop Implement Evaluate
Adult Literacy: Writing for Everyday Life
City Literacy Project — 6-module self-paced course
AudienceAdult community learners
Modules6 self-paced modules
ToolsArticulate Rise, Loom

The Challenge

Community learners needed practical writing skills for real-life contexts — job applications, emails, and forms — but existing materials were text-heavy, patronizing, and had a 30% completion rate.

My Solution

Conducted learner interviews and a needs assessment survey to inform a scenario-based e-learning course where every module centers on a realistic life situation. Leveraged plain language principles and multimedia to support varying literacy levels.

  • Completion rate increased from 30% to 70% after redesign
  • 6 scenario-based modules each with knowledge checks and reflection prompts
  • Recorded instructional video introductions using Loom for each module
  • Accessible design: captions, alt text, adjustable reading level
🎓 Prof. Development
Analyze Design Develop Implement Evaluate
Differentiated Instruction Workshop Series
Lincoln Unified School District — 8-session series
AudienceSecondary educators (40+)
Format8 x 90-min ILT sessions
ToolsSlides, Padlet, Nearpod

The Challenge

District data showed inconsistent use of differentiated instruction across secondary classrooms, with teachers citing lack of practical strategies and time as key barriers. Traditional PD days were viewed as compliance exercises, not genuine learning.

My Solution

Designed an 8-session series grounded in adult learning theory — each session opened with a real classroom challenge, modeled a specific DI strategy, provided practice time, and ended with a 2-week classroom application commitment.

  • 94% participant satisfaction rating across all 8 sessions
  • Designed all facilitator guides, slide decks, and participant workbooks
  • Built in peer observation and reflection cycles between sessions
  • Post-survey showed 80% of participants applied at least 2 new strategies

My Approach

ADDIE in practice

Every project I design follows a rigorous, iterative process. Here's what that looks like in the real world.

A

Analyze

Stakeholder interviews, learner surveys, performance gap analysis, and audience profiling before a single slide is designed.

D

Design

Learning objectives mapped to Bloom's levels, content outlines, storyboards, and assessment blueprints reviewed by SMEs.

D

Develop

Building modules in Articulate Rise or Storyline, creating facilitator guides, job aids, and multimedia assets.

I

Implement

Pilot testing with a small cohort, facilitating ILT sessions, deploying to LMS, and training facilitators on delivery.

E

Evaluate

Kirkpatrick Levels 1–3 evaluation: learner reaction surveys, knowledge assessments, and 30-day behavior transfer checks.
